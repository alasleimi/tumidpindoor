\begin{abstract}
This report integrates four paper-specific tasks rather than pooling unlike
experiments: MPUrge--MAP receiver localization, original MPUrge virtual-
transmitter (VT) construction, SMART--LEA refinement, and the two-room CNN
fingerprinting task.  Every numerical result is labelled either a completed
replacement-scene benchmark, a diagnostic smoke result, published context, or
pending evidence.  Fairness is the paper-specific number and location of
physical acquisitions, not a delay-only rule: noisy power, AoA, and complex
CIR are legal when obtained from those same acquisitions.  Hidden ray/VT IDs,
query truth, clean side channels, and extra spatial supervision are forbidden.
The report gives a beginner-reproducible recipe for every method appearing in
a table, complete leaderboards for finished protocols, explicit hooks for the
two pending full runs, and a release/audit ledger.
\end{abstract}

\section{What may and may not be claimed}

The four source papers use simulator projects, scene files, and random seeds
that are not available in this workspace.  Consequently, a number produced
here is an \emph{executable replacement-scene reconstruction}; it is not an
exact reproduction of the paper's number and not an external state-of-the-art
claim.  Published values appear only as context and are never pooled with
reconstruction rows.

The paper/reconstruction boundary is operational:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A printed paper fact is stated as such and tied to its protocol.
\item A missing choice---geometry, AP coordinate, corruption extractor,
threshold, seed, or ambiguity---is disclosed as a reconstruction choice.
\item A protected query may score a frozen method but may not select a model,
threshold, sensor channel, fallback, or ablation.
\item A method may use every noisy physical observable at the same permitted
acquisitions.  It may not add labelled positions or read latent simulator
identities, clean geometry, the true obstacle label, or query coordinates.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Shared acquisition and metric contract}

Latent paths first synthesize a noisy array impulse response,
\begin{equation}
 y_a[n]=\sum_{\ell}\alpha_\ell
 e^{-j(2\pi/\lambda)\mathbf p_a^\top\mathbf u_\ell}
 \mathbf 1[n=n_\ell]+w_a[n],\qquad
 w_a[n]\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,\sigma^2).
\end{equation}
Peak extraction yields a token
$z_\ell=(\widehat r_\ell,\widehat P_\ell,\widehat{\mathbf u}_\ell)$
and leaves the noisy complex CIR available.  Missing detection removes the
whole token, so delay, power, AoA, dropout, and CIR noise are coupled.  Survey
and query acquisitions use independent draws.  Latent source/ray identity and
reflection order are never estimator features.

For a query truth $\mathbf x_b$ and prediction $\widehat{\mathbf x}_b$,
\begin{equation}
 e_b=\|\widehat{\mathbf x}_b-\mathbf x_b\|_2,\quad
 \overline e=B^{-1}\sum_b e_b,\quad
 \operatorname{RMSE}=\sqrt{B^{-1}\sum_b e_b^2}.
\end{equation}
Median and 90th percentile are also retained.  Pairwise uncertainty uses the
same physical blocks for both methods: with
$\Delta_b=e_b^{\rm baseline}-e_b^{\rm candidate}$, a positive
$\overline\Delta$ means the candidate is better.  Blocks, not augmentation
duplicates, are resampled.

\section{Protocol I: corrected MPUrge--MAP}

\subsection{Frozen reconstruction}

Every method receives the same 80 survey locations and the same 100 locked
off-grid query locations.  The 16 cells are ideal, nine range-AWGN levels
$\sigma_r\in\{0.01,0.02,0.05,0.1,0.2,0.5,1,2,5\}\m$, five individual
objects, and all objects.  Each cell has one materialised survey tensor and an
independently corrupted query tensor shared verbatim across methods.  Learned
augmentation corrupts the 80 stored fingerprints only (1,280 rows; zero new
simulator coordinates).  Selection uses 64 whole RP groups for fit and 16 for
validation.  Seven neural methods use seeds 211, 337, and 461.  The 100 query
labels are opened only for final scoring.

The observable channels are delay, noisy received power, noisy receive AoA,
and noisy 8-element CIR.  Zero-range-noise cells use the frozen high-SNR
receiver setting (80 dB); range-AWGN cells use 25 dB for other modalities.
That sensitivity choice is part of the replacement protocol and is a reason
not to extrapolate its multimodal margin directly to hardware.

\subsection{Common equations}

For sorted query set $Q=\{q_a\}$ and RP set $R_i=\{r_{ib}\}$, corrected
MPUrge uses
\begin{align}
 \delta_{ab}&=\alpha|q_a-r_{ib}|+(1-\alpha)d_{\rm pat}(a,b),\\
 d_{\rm pat}(a,b)&=\binom{2p+1}{2}^{-1}
 \sum_{u<v}\left|,|W_a(u)-W_a(v)|-|W_{ib}(u)-W_{ib}(v)|\,\right|,\\
 C_i&=\frac{|\mathcal M_i|}{\max(|Q|,|R_i|,1)},\qquad
 s_i=\frac{1}{C_i|\mathcal M_i|}\sum_{(a,b)\in\mathcal M_i}\delta_{ab}.
\end{align}
Mutual-nearest matches are processed in increasing composite $\delta$, and a
new match may not reverse the delay order of an accepted match.  The baseline
uses $p=6$, $\alpha=0.7$, normalized pattern distance, and inverse-score
interpolation over the three best finite RPs:
\begin{equation}
 w_i=\frac{s_i^{-1}}{\sum_{j\in K}s_j^{-1}},\qquad
 \widehat{\mathbf x}=\sum_{i\in K}w_i\mathbf x_i.
\end{equation}
No finite evidence returns the survey-coordinate centroid for every method.

Three reusable decoders are
\begin{align}
 D_i^{\rm Ch}&=\tfrac12\left[|Q|^{-1}\sum_{q\in Q}\min_{r\in R_i}|q-r|
 +|R_i|^{-1}\sum_{r\in R_i}\min_{q\in Q}|r-q|\right],\\
 w_i^{(T)}&=\frac{\exp[-(s_i-s_{(1)})/T]}
 {\sum_{j\in K}\exp[-(s_j-s_{(1)})/T]},\\
 p^{(t+1)}&=(1-\gamma)p^{(t)}+\gamma G^\top p^{(t)},\qquad
 \widehat{\mathbf x}=\sum_i p_i^{(L)}\mathbf x_i,
\end{align}
where $G$ is the normalized six-neighbour RP graph.  The frozen EvoMDP row
uses $E_i=\sum_{k=1}^8w_k f_k(Q,R_i)/a_k$ for eight delay-set distances:
Chamfer, quantile Wasserstein, gap Wasserstein, log-cardinality ratio, partial
assignment, local pattern, MCA complement, and moment difference.

For multimodal matching,
\begin{align}
 c_{ab}&=|r_a-r_b|+\lambda_\theta\arccos(\mathbf u_a^\top\mathbf u_b)
 +\lambda_P|P_a-P_b|/10,\\
 z_{ab}&=c_{ab}/(1+c_{ab}),\qquad S(z)=(1-z)^2(1+2z).
\end{align}
The Beta-shaped row symmetrically averages nearest pair costs weighted by
$S(z)$ and adds a normalized cardinality mismatch.  ``Beta-shaped'' is the
correct name: the implemented polynomial is not an exact Beta$(2,5)$ survival
integral.  The coherent row uses the receiver's active-tap array projection
distance $D_i^{\rm ADP}$ plus $\lambda_dD_i^{\rm Ch}$.  Survival--CIR combines
$D_i^{\rm surv}=aD_i^{\rm Beta}+bD_i^{\rm ADP+ToA}+cD_i^{\rm Ch}$; residual
graph diffuses this score over the RP graph.

PointNet/Deep-Sets encode tokens using
\begin{equation}
 h_\ell=\phi(z_\ell),\qquad e(Z)=
 [\operatorname{mean}_\ell h_\ell,\operatorname{max}_\ell h_\ell,
 \log(1+|Z|)].
\end{equation}
CAEZ-style classification uses $p_i=\operatorname{softmax}(g)_i$ and
$\widehat{\mathbf x}=\sum_i p_i\mathbf x_i$.  A residual head uses
$\widehat{\mathbf x}=\operatorname{clip}\{\mathbf a+\rho\tanh f_\theta(e,\mathbf a,d)\}$.
RRLE predicts each frozen expert's error $\widehat e_k$ and mixes its four
coordinates with $\pi_k\propto e^{-\widehat e_k/T}$.  Genuine cross-attention
uses
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{Attn}(Q,K,V)=\operatorname{softmax}
 \left(QK^\top/\sqrt d+M\right)V,
\end{equation}
with padding mask $M$ and no path-order positional encoding.  The self-attention
candidate model applies the same masked operation within each set, pools, then
scores candidates equivariantly.  Shuffle, padding, and candidate-permutation
tests passed.

\section{Protocol II: original MPUrge VT construction}

\subsection{Corrected task and evidence boundary}

This protocol estimates an \emph{unordered set of VT coordinates}, not a
receiver coordinate.  Transmitter identity is observable because elementary
transmitters use separable waveform channels; it is retained throughout
matching, grouping, support counting, and evaluation.  True VT coordinates,
cardinality, ray identity, and query truth are never primary-method inputs.
The paper's total window $P=5$ is implemented as half-width $p=2$.  Current
conflicts are resolved in increasing composite dissimilarity, and both the
literal printed crossing test and geometric order-reversal test are recorded.
Both overlapping-star and conflict-resolved connected-component grouping are
bracketed.  Beta support is swept until the retained set is empty.

The intended full design contains 24 independent scene/noise blocks: six scene
seeds times $\sigma_r\in\{0,0.25,1,3\}\m$, 36 survey positions, 20 held-out
queries, 90 differentiable-assignment steps with two restarts, 120 epochs for
each self-supervised set model, and a $31\times31$ downstream localization
grid.  The process stopped after checkpoint stages for 7/24 blocks and wrote
no block metrics, aggregate, report, or manifest.  Those checkpoints are not a
partial leaderboard.

The completed diagnostic smoke used one scene, $\sigma_r\in\{0,0.25\}\m$,
20 survey positions, six queries, eight neural epochs, 12 assignment steps,
and a $15\times15$ grid.  Its one-scene intervals are necessarily degenerate.

\subsection{Construction and scoring equations}

For nearby survey anchors $\mathbf x_i$, match unordered indirect ranges with
the corrected MPUrge dissimilarity from Protocol I (or the PBA L2 size-
unification control).  A conflict-compatible group $\mathcal G$ is converted
to a VT by robust range trilateration,
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{\mathbf v}_{\mathcal G}=arg\min_{\mathbf v\in\R^2}
 \sum_{(i,a)\in\mathcal G}
 \left(\|\mathbf v-\mathbf x_i\|_2-r_{ia}\right)^2.
\end{equation}
For a candidate $\mathbf v$, support is the number of distinct anchors whose
measured range agrees within the frozen gate.  With mean support $\bar s$, the
descriptive sweep retains $\{\mathbf v:s(\mathbf v)\ge\beta\bar s\}$ and
increases $\beta$ until this set is empty.

Multimodal inverse proposals are
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf v_{ia}=\mathbf x_i+r_{ia}\mathbf u_{ia}.
\end{equation}
Complete-link clusters with at least three anchors are power-weighted.  The
survival version keeps a cluster only when
$\Pr[p>0.15\mid m,n]=1-F_{\mathrm{Beta}(1+m,1+n-m)}(0.15)\ge0.90$.
The Bernoulli/RFS tracker updates a matched track existence by
$q\leftarrow q+0.35(1-q)$ and a missed track by $q\leftarrow0.94q$; it emits
tracks with $q\ge0.55$ and support from at least three anchors.

Unknown-cardinality differentiable assignment tests candidate counts, uses a
soft symmetric set loss
\begin{equation}
 L_K=\frac1N\sum_i\left[
 \frac1{|R_i|}\sum_{r\in R_i}\operatorname{smin}_{k,\tau}|r-d_{ik}|+
 \frac1K\sum_k\operatorname{smin}_{r\in R_i,\tau}|d_{ik}-r|\right]
 +\operatorname{BIC}(K),
\end{equation}
where $d_{ik}=\|\mathbf x_i-\mathbf v_k\|_2$ and
$\operatorname{smin}_{\tau}(a)=-\tau\log\sum_j e^{-a_j/\tau}$.  DeepSets and
self-attention instead predict a variable-existence set and minimize only
range/AoA reconstruction on the allowed survey; no VT labels are used.

Evaluation first maximizes gated bipartite match cardinality and only then
minimizes matched distance.  If TP, FP, and FN are the resulting counts,
\begin{equation}
 \mathrm{precision}=\frac{TP}{TP+FP},\quad
 \mathrm{recall}=\frac{TP}{TP+FN},\quad
 F_1=\frac{2TP}{2TP+FP+FN}.
\end{equation}
Symmetric Chamfer, Hausdorff, and downstream grid-localization error prevent a
high F1 from hiding poor coordinates or an unusable VT set.

\section{Protocol III: SMART--LEA}

\subsection{Frozen full reconstruction}

The paper describes an irregular two-dimensional floorplan with four physical
transmitters and four first-order VTs per transmitter (20 anchors total).  The
replacement uses a disclosed $25\times25\m$ rectangle, invented Tx coordinates,
and four wall images per Tx.  A single stored $100\times100$ survey at
$0.25\m$ provides strict nested maps of 10,000, 2,500, 289, and 100 RPs for
spacings $0.25,0.5,1.5,2.5\m$.  No coarse method sees dense-map rows.  Each of
seeds 29, 47, and 71 has 1,000 fixed off-grid queries in clean and
matched-noisy conditions.  Matched noise means 20 dB receiver SNR plus
$0.25\m$ range, 2 dB power, and $5\degc$ direction perturbations after
extraction.  Learned selection holds out complete 5 m spatial blocks, then a
fresh model is fitted at the permitted spacing.

SMART's literal primary input is an initial MCA coordinate, the coordinates of
the 20 paper-known real/virtual transmitters, and the measured delay set.
Richer map challengers instead receive only their declared same-acquisition
features and RP coordinates.  The separately labelled legacy four-solve row
uses the observable physical-Tx channel partition; it is not Algorithm 1 and
is excluded from primary winner claims.

\subsection{Core equations and method recipes}

At iteration $t$, SMART recalculates every anchor range and jointly matches all
20 values:
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde r_j^{(t)}=\|\widetilde{\mathbf x}^{(t)}-\mathbf v_j\|_2,
 \qquad
 \widetilde{\mathbf x}^{(t+1)}=arg\min_{\mathbf x}
 \sum_{(a,j)\in\mathcal M^{(t)}}
 \left(\|\mathbf x-\mathbf v_j\|_2-q_a\right)^2.
\end{equation}
The fixed-height correction evaluates
$\sqrt{(x-v_{jx})^2+(y-v_{jy})^2+(z_r-v_{jz})^2}$ in both matching and least
squares.  Total $P=5$ means half-window $p=2$.  The printed crossing test
$(q_a-r_b)(q_c-r_d)<0$ and geometric test
$(q_a-q_c)(r_b-r_d)<0$ are separate rows.

The additional analytic scores reuse MCA, Chamfer, graph diffusion, subset
consensus, and EvoMDP from Protocol I.  The combined delay score is
\begin{equation}
 D_i=\beta\widetilde D_i^{\rm Ch}+(1-\beta)
 \left[\tfrac12\widetilde D_i^{W_1}+
 \tfrac12\left(1-S_i^{\rm MCA}/\max_jS_j^{\rm MCA}\right)\right].
\end{equation}
For unit-normalized array-PDP $\mathbf c$, coherent distance is
$D_i^{\rm CIR}=1-\mathbf c_q^\top\mathbf c_i$; delay--CIR uses
$\eta\widetilde D_i^{\rm Ch}+(1-\eta)\widetilde D_i^{\rm CIR}$.
Multimodal assignment uses Hungarian matching with
\begin{equation}
 C_{ab}=|r_a-r_b|/(0.5\m)+|P_a-P_b|/(3\,\mathrm{dB})+
 \arccos(\mathbf u_a^\top\mathbf u_b)/(10\degc),
\end{equation}
plus an unmatched-path penalty.

Survey-built VTs minimize a robust range residual over persistent tracks,
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{\mathbf v}_k=\arg\min_{v_x,v_y,h\ge0}
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal T_k}\rho\!\left(
 \sqrt{(x_i-v_x)^2+(y_i-v_y)^2+h^2}-r_{ik}\right).
\end{equation}
The differentiable variant alternately Sinkhorn-normalizes
$A_{iak}\propto e^{-|r_{ia}-d_{ik}|/\tau}$ and minimizes the robust
assignment-weighted range loss.  True VTs are used only after fitting for an
audit error.

\section{Protocol IV: two-room CNN fingerprinting}

\subsection{Printed protocol and corrected reconstruction}

The paper uses Room A ($20\times8\m$) and Room B ($16\times12\m$), 20 RPs,
60 AP positions, and seven obstacle configurations.  This grants
$20\times60\times7=8{,}400$ labelled calibration acquisitions per room.  It
tests four obstacle-count conditions, two APs per condition, and 400 uniform
points per condition: 1,600 locked off-grid queries per room.  The printed
input contains the nine ranges selected by \emph{observed} power, all 20 RP
coordinate triplets, and the AP coordinate, reshaped to $6\times12$.

The replacement preserves those counts and room sizes, but its disclosed AP
positions, obstacle planes, and image-source propagation are not NIST Q-D.
Each legal challenger receives no more than the printed 8,400 acquisitions;
it may use noisy delay, power, receive AoA, and array CIR extracted there.  It
may not add positions, simulations, clean channels, ray/VT IDs, query truth, or
hidden obstacle identity in the deployable bracket.

Within each AP/configuration group, 16 of 20 RP acquisitions fit and four
validate.  Candidate/residual/RRLE pseudo-queries compare only with fit-map
rows and explicitly exclude the source acquisition:
\begin{equation}
 j\notin\mathcal C(Q_j),\qquad \mathcal C(Q_j)\subseteq\mathcal I_{\rm fit}.
\end{equation}
Locked queries may use the complete acquired map only after all choices freeze.

Reference methods have two non-equivalent information brackets.  The
paper-matched sensitivity bracket supplies the true obstacle condition
$R_i^{\rm oracle}=R_{aic_q}$ and is explicitly privileged.  The deployable
environment-blind bracket selects, per RP,
\begin{equation}
 c_i^*=\arg\min_{c\in\{0,\ldots,6\}}D(Q,R_{aic}),\qquad
 R_i^{\rm blind}=R_{aic_i^*},
\end{equation}
without exposing the label.  Direct models use neither bracket and are emitted
once as protocol-independent rows.

\subsection{Equations and the 25-method recipe index}

Fit-only min--max scaling converts observed-power-ranked delays to
$\widetilde d_{1:9}$.  The paper vector
\begin{equation}
 v=[\widetilde d_{1:9},\widetilde{\mathbf x}_{1:20}^{\rm RP},
 \widetilde{\mathbf a}]\in\R^{72}
\end{equation}
is reshaped column-major to $1\times6\times12$.  The classifier outputs
$p_i=\operatorname{softmax}(g)_i$ and
$\widehat{\mathbf x}=\sum_i p_i\mathbf x_i$ with cross-entropy; the regressor
minimizes the printed batch coordinate RMSE.  Three $2\times1$ convolution
blocks yield 36,148 classifier and 36,190 regressor parameters; the paper's
reported regressor count differs by 21 and remains unresolved.

Map methods reuse MCA, corrected MPUrge, Chamfer, subset consensus, graph
diffusion, and EvoMDP from Protocol I.  Multimodal pair costs are
\begin{equation}
 C_{ab}^{rP}=|r_a-r_b|+|P_a-P_b|/8,qquad
 C_{ab}^{r\theta}=|r_a-r_b|+1.5\arccos(\mathbf u_a^\top\mathbf u_b).
\end{equation}
For active CIR taps, coherent ADP uses
\begin{equation}
 \rho_n=\frac{|\mathbf q_n^H\mathbf r_n|^2}

\subsection{Confirmed positive causal sequential result}

G2 wraps the frozen corrected-MPUrge RP score vector.  Rank scores form the
emission and surveyed RP distances form the transition:
\begin{align}
 \ell_t(i)&=\exp\!\left[-\beta\,
 \frac{\operatorname{rank}(s_t(i))}{N-1}\right],\\
 T_{ij}&\propto\exp[-\|\mathbf x_i-\mathbf x_j\|_2^2/(2\sigma_T^2)],\\
 p_t&=\operatorname{normalize}\{\ell_t\odot(T^\top p_{t-1})\},\qquad
 \widehat{\mathbf x}_t=\sum_{i\in\operatorname{top}K(p_t)}
 \frac{p_t(i)}{\sum_{j\in K}p_t(j)}\mathbf x_i.
\end{align}
Earlier frames froze $\beta=8$, $\sigma_T=.6\m$, zero extra stay weight, and
$K=5$ before three shifted 200-frame confirmation segments were opened.  The
predictor uses only past belief, current analytic scores, and RP coordinates;
no future observation or query coordinate enters it.

\subsection{Minimal reproducibility checklist}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Materialize and hash survey/query observations before training.  Verify
independent survey/query corruption and absence of forbidden identity fields.
\item Enforce the exact paper-specific acquisition count.  At sparse SMART
spacing, subset the stored dense acquisition before any fit; for CNN, never
exceed the printed 8,400 acquisitions per room.
\item Fit normalization and choose all hyperparameters on grouped calibration
only.  Exclude the pseudo-query's source row from any reference-conditioned
training/validation candidate set.
\item Freeze configuration, source hashes, seed list, fallbacks, brackets, and
method registry before opening locked queries.  Keep learned seeds separate in
raw evidence before ensembling.
\item Save prediction coordinates or VT sets, scoring truth, physical block
keys, runtime, feature provenance, map-information bracket, checkpoints, and
hashes.  Reconstruct every reported error mechanically from raw rows.
\item Run empty/no-evidence, padding, path-shuffle, candidate-shuffle, and
finite-output tests.  Cluster paired intervals by physical query/AP/scene, not
augmentation row.
\item Label every number as paper context, full reconstruction, diagnostic
smoke, oracle sensitivity, or pending.  Never use a completed task's number to
fill another task's missing table.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Bottom line}

On the completed MAP replacement, the best dependable row is the
same-acquisition range/power/AoA Beta-shaped survival adapter at $0.2528\m$
mean, versus $1.0638\m$ for corrected prose-consistent Majid.  Survival--CIR
and the multimodal residual graph are close; permutation-invariant
self-attention and genuine cross-attention are also strong.  Controlled
feature ablations show that most of this margin comes from richer noisy sensing,
not attention alone.

On SMART, the dominant method depends sharply on map density and receiver
noise.  Fixed-height geometric LEA4 is exceptionally accurate on dense clean
maps but has a rare severe tail at 0.5 m; multimodal assignment dominates
sparser maps, delay--CIR wins the densest noisy map, and multimodal attention
wins noisy 0.5 m.  The all-cell table must accompany any headline.

Original MPUrge VT construction and corrected CNN performance remain open
release items.  The report supplies exact recipes and input hooks but does not
convert incomplete runs into claims.  Separately, the causal analytic G2
filter is a confirmed positive sequential extension, whereas honest active
multilevel calibration is neutral under paired uncertainty.
